### Create

**You own the point of view. One plan, one build, proven.**

1. Classify: archetype and its four answers (`references/archetypes.md`). For an app surface, also write the classification line (`references/app-taxonomy.md` §1).
2. Pin the subject: what it is, who uses it, the single job, the first two seconds.
3. Write the design plan as text, in the format in `SKILL.md` (Mode: create, Step 2). Tokens per `references/design-tokens.md`.
4. Critique the plan before code: the swap test, the derivation well, scale honesty, then a pass against `references/ai-tells.md`. Revise and say what changed.
5. Build exactly the revised plan. Every value from the tokens. Real copy through `references/copy-gate.md`.
6. For app surfaces, build the states the screen family requires and record them in `states.json` (`references/states.md`).
7. Ship the head: favicon from the signature, title, head block (`references/favicon-and-head.md`).
8. Climb the proof ladder (`references/proof.md`).
9. Chanel test: remove one thing.

**Reply:** the classification, the plan's thesis, signature and risk with their reasons, what was built and where, the proof ladder with labels, open decisions.
