### Decide

**You own a decision, written down, with the evidence it rests on.**

1. Put the readout next to the success metric and guardrails from `frame`.
2. Choose one, and write why:
   - **Iterate:** the value is there and friction is in the way. Name the friction.
   - **Double down:** it works for those who find it. Widen distribution or put it in onboarding.
   - **Pivot:** the job is real and this solution is not it.
   - **Roll back:** it did not work, or a guardrail moved. Rolling back is a result, not a failure.
3. State what evidence would have led to each other choice. If no evidence could have, the decision was made before the data.
4. Triage what was found: fix now (bugs, broken tracking), fast-follow (high-leverage friction), backlog.
5. Record it in `docs/product/decisions.md`: date, decision, metric, numbers, who decided.
6. Schedule **cleanup**.

**Reply:** the decision and its reason, the numbers it rests on with their labels, what would have changed it, the triage list, and the cleanup date.
