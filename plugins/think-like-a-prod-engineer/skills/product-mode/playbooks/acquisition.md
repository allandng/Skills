### Acquisition

**You own which sources bring users who stay. Volume is someone else's number.**

1. Confirm source is captured at sign-up, set once, and survives the path through app stores and redirects. If not, fix that first (**instrument**).
2. For each source: sign-ups, activation rate, and retention at the natural period, with n and intervals (the `product-analytics` skill's funnel and retention scripts, segmented by source).
3. Rank sources by activated-and-retained users, not sign-ups.
4. Check the mix before the cause: do sources differ in platform, plan or region? (the `metric-check` skill.)
5. For the worst-retaining large source, look at what those users were promised. A mismatch between the promise and the first screen is the usual reason.
6. Say what cannot be seen: untracked sources, dark traffic, sign-ups with a blank source, and how large that share is.

**Reply:** the source table (sign-ups, activation, retention, n), the ranking by retained users, mix differences, the promise-to-product gap for the worst source, and the share of unattributed sign-ups.
