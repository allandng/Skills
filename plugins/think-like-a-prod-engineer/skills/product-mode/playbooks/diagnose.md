### Diagnose

**You own finding where the problem is before anyone proposes what to do about it.**

1. Write what moved, by how much, since when, as originally defined. Then check it is real (the `metric-check` skill): a tracking change, a definition change, a release, a season.
2. Split active users into new, retained and resurrected, and the loss into churned (`references/lifecycle.md`). Which part moved?
3. Split by platform, and on mobile by app version. A drop confined to one version is a bug, not a trend.
4. Split by source and by cohort week. A drop confined to recent cohorts is acquisition or onboarding. One across all cohorts is the product or the season.
5. Walk the lifecycle transitions in order and find the one with the largest change. That is the constraint.
6. Stop splitting when a split explains most of the change. Report that one. Do not report ten.
7. Hand off to the playbook for that transition: **onboarding**, **funnel-drop**, **retention**, **engagement** or **acquisition**.

**Reply:** what moved and that it is real, the part of the user base responsible, the platform, version, source and cohort where it concentrates, the constraint, and the next playbook.
